# DRAG V-16
## Fixed-Wing UAV for Medical Logistics

*Connecting Healthcare. Delivering Critical Supplies.*

DRAG V-16 is a fixed-wing unmanned aerial vehicle (UAV) developed by Dragale Aviation Private Limited for medical logistics and time-sensitive healthcare transportation. With a payload capacity of 16 kg, the platform is designed to support the transportation of essential medical supplies between hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, blood banks and healthcare facilities.

Its design focuses on payload capacity, range and operational efficiency to support point-to-point medical logistics missions.

### Medical Mission Applications

*Blood & Blood Products*  
Transportation of blood units and eligible blood products between blood banks, hospitals and healthcare facilities.

*Diagnostic Laboratory Samples*  
Transportation of diagnostic specimens and laboratory samples between collection centres, diagnostic laboratories and hospitals.

*Vaccine Transportation*  
Support for vaccine distribution using appropriate packaging and validated temperature-control systems.

*Medicines & Pharmaceuticals*  
Transportation of medicines and pharmaceutical supplies between healthcare facilities and distribution points.

*Emergency Medical Supplies*  
Delivery of selected emergency medical supplies and essential healthcare materials.

*Medical Logistics Networks*  
Aerial transportation solutions for hospitals, diagnostic laboratories, blood banks and healthcare networks.
